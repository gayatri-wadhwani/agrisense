07 / Safety by design
Decision support, not a diagnosis engine.
Safety is not a feature; it is the foundation. Agrisense is designed to provide field intelligence that empowers farmers to consult experts, rather than replacing them with unverified AI certainty.

Confidence Framing

Every AI insight is presented with explicit confidence levels and "possible issue" language to prevent over-reliance.

Referral Guidance

Immediate actions always include a recommendation to verify findings with local agricultural officers or experts.

No Unsafe Certainty

The system strictly avoids definitive disease claims and unsafe pesticide prescriptions or dosages.
