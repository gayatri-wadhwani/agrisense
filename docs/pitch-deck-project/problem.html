02 / The problem
The field is full of signals. The farmer still has to guess.
Small and marginal farmers often make high-consequence crop decisions with fragmented weather, soil, and plant-health information.
The gap is not information. It is timely, localised, explainable guidance.

Weather windows

SIGNAL 02

Soil stress

SIGNAL 03

Plant health
